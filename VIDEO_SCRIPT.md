# WordPress Job Introduction Video Script

**Target Length:** 2-3 minutes
**Style:** Authentic walkthrough + professional business value
**Goal:** Showcase technical expertise while demonstrating business impact

---

## **Opening Hook** (15-20 seconds)

### **Visual:** Professional headshot or portfolio logo

**Script:**
"Hey there! My name is Kelvin Perez, and I'm a Senior Full-Stack WordPress Developer with over 8 years of experience building high-performance web solutions that drive real business results."

### **Why this works:**
- ✅ Confident, direct introduction
- ✅ Establishes credibility immediately
- ✅ Focuses on business value ("real business results")

---

## **Technical Checklist for Recording**

### **Before Recording:**
- [ ] Test all website demos for speed and functionality
- [ ] Prepare browser with necessary tabs open
- [ ] Check microphone quality and lighting
- [ ] Practice transitions between talking and demos

### **During Recording:**
- [ ] Speak clearly and confidently
- [ ] Maintain eye contact with camera
- [ ] Use mouse pointer to highlight key elements
- [ ] Keep demos concise and impactful
- [ ] Stay within 2-3 minute target

### **Key Technical Points to Emphasize:**
1. **WordPress Expertise:** Custom post types, ACF Pro, dynamic content
2. **Performance:** 100/100 PageSpeed, 1ms response times, Core Web Vitals
3. **Business Impact:** 487 pages, $50K-$100K SEO value, automation
4. **Modern Stack:** PHP 8.3+, modern JavaScript, TypeScript
5. **Full-Stack Capability:** Design → Development → Deployment → Optimization

---

## **Business Value Statements**

### **Replace Technical Jargon with Business Impact:**

- Say: "I created an automated system that generates 487 pages"

### **Instead of:** "I optimized Core Web Vitals"
### **Say:** "I achieved 100/100 PageSpeed scores, meaning faster load times and better user experience"

### **Instead of:** "I built a dynamic sitemap"
### **Say:** "I ensured Google indexes all our content, driving organic traffic"

---

## **Troubleshooting Common Issues**

### **If demos lag:**
- Have screenshots ready as backup
- Pre-load all websites in browser tabs
- Consider using Loom for post-production editing

### **If running long:**
- Cut the books/gear section
- Shorten the timeline talk
- Focus on 1-2 key projects instead of many

### **If technical issues:**
- Have portfolio images ready
- Be prepared to describe instead of show
- Keep calm and professional

---

## **Final Tips**

1. **Be Authentic:** Let your personality show through
2. **Focus on Value:** Every technical feature should connect to business benefit
3. **Be Specific:** Use real numbers and metrics
4. **Stay Confident:** You're an expert developer with impressive results
5. **End Strong:** Clear call to action and contact information

Remember: You're not just a WordPress developer - you're a business problem solver who happens to use WordPress as your tool. Let that confidence shine through!
