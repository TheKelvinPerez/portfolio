## Frontmatter Field Reference

- **title** (string, required): Post title, max 99 characters
- **description** (string, required): Brief summary, max 999 characters
- **date** (string, required): Publication date in ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **imageUrl** (string, required): Path to featured image
- **published** (boolean, optional): Whether to publish the post (default: true)
- **featured** (boolean, optional): Whether to show in featured section (default: false)
- **tags** (array of strings, optional): List of topic tags

## Tips for Writing Blog Posts

1. Keep titles under 99 characters for better SEO
2. Write descriptions that are 2-3 sentences
3. Use descriptive tags to categorize content
4. Set `featured: true` for your best posts
5. Use `published: false` for drafts
6. Test all MDX components before publishing
7. Optimize images before adding (WebP format, < 200KB)
